Clinton Watson

Physiotherapist / Director

Clinton graduated from La Trobe University in 1994 and has spent the last 30 years working in private practice on the Mornington Peninsula, as well as stints with Dandenong Stingrays and Frankston Dolphins Football Clubs and the Victorian Institute of Sport Triathlon program.

Whilst seeing the benefits of manual therapy, Clinton is determined to bring a holistic approach to physiotherapy, looking at ways to enhance the body's natural healing properties, assessing movement patterns and biomechanical issues that may contribute to an injury, and focusing on both short and long term goals.

Clinton brings a very patient centred approach to injury management combining his coaching skills, personal experience, and a desire to help others into his daily practice. With a specific interest in running and triathlon injury management, Clinton has the tools to take full control of all aspects of your return to activity.

Qualifications

Bachelor of Physiotherapy (La Trobe University, 1994)

Areas of focus

Running and triathlon injury management, holistic physiotherapy, biomechanical assessment
