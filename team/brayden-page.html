Brayden Page

Physiotherapist / Director

Brayden is a dedicated and experienced physiotherapist with a genuine passion for helping people move better, recover fully, and build strength for long-term health and performance.

His approach combines detailed assessment, hands-on treatment, and tailored exercise programs to create lasting, meaningful results for each patient.

With formal qualifications including a Master of Physiotherapy and a Bachelor of Sport and Exercise Science (First Class Honours), Brayden brings a deep understanding of movement, performance, and rehabilitation to his practice.

He has developed particular interest in managing running and strength-training injuries, spinal and postural pain, and post-surgical rehabilitation. Brayden also has a strong interest in golf-related physiotherapy, enjoying the process of analysing individual anatomy and movement and how it relates to swing mechanics and ultimately helping golfers of all levels recover from injury, improve mobility, and enhance their game.

Brayden has spent the majority of his career working in Mornington, and is very excited to continue working on the Mornington Peninsula in his hometown Dromana.

Outside the clinic, Brayden enjoys golf, running, basketball, snowboarding, and spending time with family and friends on the Mornington Peninsula.

Qualifications

Master of Physiotherapy, Bachelor of Sport and Exercise Science (First Class Honours)

Areas of focus

Running injuries, spinal and postural pain, post-surgical rehabilitation, golf-related physiotherapy
