What to expect

At Hillview Physiotherapy Group, we aim to make your experience as comfortable and straightforward as possible. From your first contact to your last appointment, our team is here to guide you through every step of your recovery.

We believe that good physiotherapy starts with understanding — understanding your goals, your history, and what matters most to you. That is why we take the time to listen carefully before creating a personalised plan for your recovery.

First appointment

During your first appointment, your physiotherapist will conduct a thorough assessment to understand your condition, discuss your goals, and begin treatment. Initial appointments typically last 45 to 60 minutes, allowing enough time for a comprehensive evaluation and starting your treatment plan.

Wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that allows us to easily assess and treat the affected area. For lower limb concerns, shorts are ideal. For shoulder or upper back issues, a singlet or loose top works well.

What to bring

To help us provide the best possible care, please bring the following to your appointment:

  • Any relevant scans, X-rays, or imaging reports
  • Reports from specialists or other health professionals
  • Referral letters if claiming through Medicare, WorkCover, TAC, NDIS, or DVA
  • Your private health insurance card
  • Comfortable clothing that allows movement

Funding

We accept a range of funding options to make physiotherapy accessible:

  • Private Health Insurance — On-the-spot claiming via HICAPS for all major health funds
  • Medicare EPC — Enhanced Primary Care (EPC) plans, also known as Chronic Disease Management plans, referred by your GP
  • WorkCover — Work-related injury management
  • TAC — Transport Accident Commission claims
  • NDIS — Self- and plan-managed participants
  • DVA — Department of Veterans Affairs

Health funds

We are registered with all major private health insurers and offer HICAPS on-the-spot claiming at our clinic. This means you can claim your rebate immediately after your appointment, with no paperwork required on your end.
