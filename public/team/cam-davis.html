Cam Davis

Physiotherapist / Director

Cam is an experienced and passionate physiotherapist who graduated from Monash University in 2021 with a Bachelor of Physiotherapy (First Class Honours). He combines evidence-based practice with strong manual therapy and exercise rehabilitation skills to support his patients in achieving their best outcomes.

Cam has a particular interest in supporting youth athletes across a range of sports with rehabilitation and performance. He has previously worked with the Frankston Blues NBL1 basketball teams, the Peninsula Waves VNL netball club, and the Peninsula Old Boys Football Club.

He also has extensive experience in post-operative rehabilitation, spinal and shoulder pain, and lower limb injuries.

Born and raised on the Mornington Peninsula, Cam is passionate about helping his local community stay active and healthy. Outside the clinic, he enjoys routinely playing basketball, tennis, golf, pickleball, and snowboarding, and is a keen follower of AFL, NBA, and cricket.
