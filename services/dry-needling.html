Dry Needling

Targeted dry needling to help release muscle tension, reduce pain, and support your rehabilitation.

What is Dry Needling?

Dry needling is a technique used to release muscle tension, reduce pain, and support your rehabilitation. Fine needles are inserted into trigger points to help muscles relax and restore normal function.

Who it helps

People experiencing muscle tension, myofascial pain, sports injuries, and those looking to support their overall physiotherapy treatment.

What to expect

Dry needling is performed by trained physiotherapists as part of your treatment plan. We will discuss the approach with you and answer any questions before proceeding.

Hillview's approach

We use dry needling as part of an integrated treatment approach. It is never used in isolation — always alongside assessment, exercise prescription, and other evidence-based techniques.
