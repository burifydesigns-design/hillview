About Hillview Physiotherapy Group

Founded by a team of passionate local clinicians, Hillview Physiotherapy Group unites three physiotherapists and a practice manager with years of experience serving the Mornington Peninsula community.

Our goal is simple — to provide the highest-quality physiotherapy experience in a supportive, modern setting.

At Hillview Physiotherapy Group, we're proud to be local physiotherapists dedicated to helping our community move and live with confidence.

Our experienced team delivers premium, evidence-based care in a purpose-built Dromana clinic featuring private consultation rooms, a fully equipped rehab gym, and a calm, welcoming environment.

We believe in clear plans, consistent progress checks, and communication that makes sense — because your recovery matters to us.
